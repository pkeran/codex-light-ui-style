# Review Rubric

Use this file for final review or when a user asks whether a screen really matches the Codex-light design language.

## Visual Language

1. Does the page feel cold-white first and blue second?
2. Is blue being used as emphasis rather than as the dominant surface color?
3. Do cards read as workbench modules rather than promo tiles?

## Structure

4. Is hierarchy coming more from borders, spacing, and typography than from heavy shadow?
5. Are gradients limited to broad background treatment rather than common cards?
6. Are active states obvious without glowing or screaming for attention?

## Components

7. Do buttons, cards, nav items, inputs, and icons belong to the same visual family?
8. Are hover / active / focus / disabled states coherent across the page?
9. Does any component look like it belongs to a different aesthetic system?

## App Fitness

10. Does the screen feel appropriate for its app surface type: overview, workbench, settings, logs, or form?
11. Is the interface calm enough for repeated operational use?
12. Would a user describe the page as “clean and professional” rather than “flashy” or “overdesigned”?

## Failure Conditions

The page is drifting away from the target style if:

- the first impression is “blue”
- the first impression is “glow”
- the first impression is “landing page”
- surface hierarchy depends on deep shadows
- cards feel decorative instead of structural
