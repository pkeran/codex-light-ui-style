# Writing Tone

Use this file when the task also changes labels, headings, summaries, badges, helper text, or empty-state copy.

## Tone

- Calm
- Operational
- Precise
- Low-drama
- Short

The writing should feel like a professional tool, not a marketing site.

## Headings

- Prefer short, functional headings
- Use concrete nouns and actions
- Avoid slogan-like copy

Good:

- Account Pool
- Export Files
- Recent Activity
- Repair Failures

Bad:

- Unlock Your Workflow Potential
- Power Up Your Export Journey

## Secondary Copy

- Keep helper text short and purposeful
- Explain what the user can do next
- Avoid fluff, hype, and filler adjectives

Good:

- Review failed imports and retry valid items.
- Export history and failure details remain available here.

Bad:

- Experience a seamless and delightful next-generation export workflow.

## Metrics and Badges

- Prefer direct labels and counts
- Keep status words short
- Use blue emphasis sparingly around numbers or active labels

Good:

- 12 accounts
- 3 failures
- Ready

Bad:

- Fully Activated
- Premium Workflow Enabled
