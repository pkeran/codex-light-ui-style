# Quality Gates

Run through this checklist before claiming a Codex-light restyle is done.

## Palette and Surfaces

- [ ] Background remains cold white or pale blue-white
- [ ] Blue is used mainly for emphasis, not as the main page fill
- [ ] Cards remain mostly pure white
- [ ] Standard cards do not use visible gradient fills

## Hierarchy and Depth

- [ ] Borders provide clear structure between surfaces
- [ ] Shadows are light and not doing all the hierarchy work
- [ ] No unnecessary glow, bloom, or neon treatment is present

## Components

- [ ] Default buttons are calmer than primary buttons
- [ ] Primary buttons use solid blue with white text
- [ ] Navigation active states are pale-blue based rather than dark-blue slabs
- [ ] Icons follow a consistent default/active color system
- [ ] Card radii feel consistent across the page
- [ ] Inputs, drawers, tables, and detail panes still belong to the same surface family

## Readability and Noise

- [ ] Primary and secondary text remain clearly differentiated
- [ ] Dense screens stay readable without becoming visually empty
- [ ] No section feels like a marketing hero inside a utility workflow
- [ ] Decorative treatment never competes with operational content

## Interaction and States

- [ ] Hover states are visible but restrained
- [ ] Focus states are precise rather than glowy
- [ ] Active states are obvious without looking neon
- [ ] Disabled states remain understandable without collapsing contrast too far

## App Surface Fit

- [ ] The chosen visual treatment matches the surface type: overview, workbench, settings, history, form, or inspector
- [ ] The screen would still feel comfortable during repeated daily use

## Final Smell Test

- [ ] The screen feels like a professional workbench
- [ ] The screen does not feel like a promo page
- [ ] The screen does not feel like a neon AI dashboard
- [ ] The screen does not feel glassy or over-styled
